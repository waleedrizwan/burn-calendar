## First principle

A hackathon entry is judged in minutes by people who have not seen the work. What they understand in that time is the result, whatever was built.

## What fails without this task

A working app is submitted late, incomplete, or without a story that shows why it matters.

## Description

Prepare the demo around a real fire event in a pilot region, fill in the Project page, and submit. The format rules are in `docs/rules.md`. Submit a first complete version early, because the page can be resubmitted until the deadline and only the last saved version is judged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The demo script follows a real fire event in a pilot region
- [ ] The demo is a slide deck of at most seven slides or a video of at most 30 seconds
- [ ] A slide deck shows the team name, the participant's name and the challenge selected
- [ ] The demo and all linked material are in English, and show nobody under 18
- [ ] The demo link and the final project link open in a private browser window without a login
- [ ] Every Project page field is filled: name, summary, demo link, project link, detailed description, AI use, NASA data, references
- [ ] The NASA Data field names the FIRMS products used, and the references field matches `docs/references.md`
- [ ] The README has setup steps and screenshots
- [ ] The README and the Detailed Project Description explain the harmonization, its validation result and its limits, including that a hotspot detection is not a fire
- [ ] No new features are started after Sunday, November 15 at 6:00 p.m. Eastern
- [ ] A first submission with the repository link and a draft summary is saved by Sunday, November 15 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern
- [ ] A first complete version is submitted by Sunday, November 15 at 8:30 p.m. Eastern
- [ ] The final version is submitted before Sunday, November 15 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern, and the confirmation banner is saved as a screenshot
